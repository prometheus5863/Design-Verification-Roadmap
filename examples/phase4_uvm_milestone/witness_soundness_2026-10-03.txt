SECTION 5 -- SCARCITY AND SOUNDNESS ARE INDEPENDENT AXES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  quadrant           witness bin                 n    admis
  scarce + SOUND     transitions bin 9           1        1
  scarce + UNSOUND   g_first bin 7               1        0
  abundant + SOUND   transitions bin 3          84       83
  [PASS] three quadrants are populated, so neither predicate implies the other
        a scarce bin can be perfectly sound (transitions 9, sole witness 0x55,
        ADMISSIBLE) and an abundant bin can in principle be unsound.  So
        10-02's witness-count item and this one are two separate audits, and
        running the first would not have found what Section 3 found.

  The fourth quadrant (abundant + UNSOUND) is EMPTY in this data.  That
  is reported, not claimed impossible: it needs a bin all of whose many
  witnesses are borderline, and there are only two borderline bytes in
  the whole population.  A DUT with a wider borderline band would have
  one, and then no scarcity scan at any threshold would find it.

  AND SOUNDNESS SUBSUMES THE EXCLUSION RULE ITSELF.  With no exclusion
  the unsound g_first bins are [7, 8, 9]: bin 7 by two BORDERLINE
  witnesses, bins 8 and 9 by one INADMISSIBLE witness each -- and bins 8
  and 9 are exactly what v8 removed by hand as illegal_bins.  So the
  nine-byte exclusion is the special case of this audit that handles
  INADMISSIBLE witnesses, and bin 7 is the case it cannot reach, because
  a borderline byte is legal to draw.  An audit written on soundness
  would have produced v8's illegal_bins AND bin 7 in one pass.
  [PASS] the no-exclusion unsound set is {7, 8, 9}, and 8 and 9 are v8's own illegal_bins
        so this instrument re-derives the hand-made exclusion as a corollary,
        which is the strongest available evidence that it is measuring the
        right property rather than a new one invented to fit 0x40

        the cell count, not the grid, not the closure cost -- distinguishes a

        log does.  The vplan must therefore name WHICH option it took; the
        coverage report cannot carry that information.

        W0 mean 473, W1 mean 499 over 40 seeds.  Removing the hardest cell

        comparison is exactly the figure a revision would be tempted to quote,
        and 10-02's own 703->663 'closes faster' line rests on the same single
        seed -- it hedged correctly, and this is the measurement behind the
