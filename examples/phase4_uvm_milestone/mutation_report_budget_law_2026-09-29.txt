WHY A MUTATION TEST ON A CHECK THAT ALREADY PASSES
--------------------------------------------------
budget_law_exhaustive.py reports 11/11 PASS against the real observer.  A
self-checking suite that passes tells you nothing about its own sensitivity,
and this one is about to carry a sign-off criterion (vplan v7) that names two
bytes out of 256.  On 2026-09-17 a bench in this repository passed 55/55
against deliberately broken RTL; on 2026-09-28 a mutant was too weak to break
what it targeted and the insensitivity was reported as a result.  So every
mutant below carries a POSITIVE CONTROL that the mutation actually arrived --
asserted against the mutated source TEXT before the suite is run -- per this
morning's rule that a mutation which does not arrive is indistinguishable, in
the output, from a system that does not respond.

METHOD
------
Each mutant is injected into a COPY of examples/phase4_uvm_milestone/
uart_uvm_tb.py in scratch space outside the repository, never into the
repository's own file.  budget_law_exhaustive.py extracts its classes from
whichever uart_uvm_tb.py sits beside it, so copying both files into a scratch
directory mutates the thing under test while leaving the checker untouched.

3 of 3 mutants detected.  Two results live in the PATTERN rather than in the
count, and both are more useful than the score:

1. M3 IS CAUGHT BY ONE CHECK, AND IT IS THE EXACTLY-KNOWN-VALUE CHECK.
   Moving the sampling point a tenth of a bit off centre leaves every byte
   decoding correctly and leaves all 512 measured budget limits inside their
   1 bp match window -- because the budget is set by the integer assignment and
   not by the sampling margin.  The only thing that notices is V1's requirement
   that the margin be EXACTLY 0.5 bit at eps = 0.  A plausible-range check on
   the margin would have accepted 0.4 without comment.  Same shape as the
   graphene repository's 2026-09-17 finding, and the argument for keeping at
   least one exactly-known value in every suite.

2. M1 DOES NOT MOVE THE CRITERION-BEARING CHECKS AT ALL.  Removing the clamp
   changes the fast side of the 128-byte g_first = 1 class and nothing else:
   V3's law, V4's containment set and V6's anchored threshold are bit-identical
   with and without it.  So that one line is load-bearing for V7's
   one-sidedness result and irrelevant to the claim vplan v7 rests on -- which
   is worth knowing in the direction that matters, because it means v7's
   two-byte set does not depend on it.

WHAT IS NOT COVERED
-------------------
These three mutants touch the DECODE.  Nothing here mutates the stimulus
generator in budget_law_exhaustive.py itself (frame_edges / bit_ps_for): a
defect there would move the model and the simulation together only if the
simulation shared it, and V6 -- which anchors one derived threshold against
the committed simulation log -- is the only check that would notice.  A mutant
on frame_edges is the obvious next one and is recorded as not done.
