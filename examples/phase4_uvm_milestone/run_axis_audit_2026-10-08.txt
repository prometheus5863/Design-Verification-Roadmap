  THE REPAIR IS DONE (2026-10-08).  The collector keeps a bounded
  set of distinct signatures per cell and Section 2b reads it, so
  Section 2's prefix numbers are no longer the best this audit can
  do -- they are kept because the gap between prefix and exact is
  itself the measurement of what the repair bought.

  WHAT IS STILL BOUNDED, and it is a different bound.  The
  signature set is capped at SIGNATURE_KEEP per cell, so a cell
  whose stimulus keeps varying past the cap is reported BOUNDED and
  its diversity is still a lower bound.  The difference from the
  witness prefix is that the cap REPORTS hitting itself, per cell,
  with a count of what it refused.  That is the whole of the
  improvement: not an unbounded log, but a bound that cannot be
  mistaken for a measurement.

  Two things this audit also does NOT claim:
    - it does not claim the collector is wrong.  Reporting 100.0 %
      bin coverage for a directed suite that closes each bin with
      one payload is an ACCURATE report of what was run.  The
      finding is about what the number licenses a reader to
      believe, which is the 2026-10-05 fault (a figure printed
      beside the thing it does not measure) one axis over.
    - it does not claim a diversity TARGET.  Choosing one is a
      reviewer's decision of the same kind as F7's goal and the
      cross-gating decision, and is left open deliberately.
    - and it does not claim the stimulus is now diverse.  Nothing
      about the suite changed today; only the measurement did.  The
      diversity-1 cells are still diversity-1 and are now known to
      be, which makes constrained-random stimulus from a UVM
      sequence a better-posed item than it was yesterday rather
      than a closed one.
