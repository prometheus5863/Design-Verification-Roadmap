  WHAT TO READ IN THE KILLER COLUMN.  Each mutant should be killed
  by the ONE check written for it, and if a mutant is killed by
  UVM_ERROR alone -- with no W-x tag -- then the audit did not
  detect it and something downstream did, which is a weaker
  detection and is worth the same scepticism 2026-10-04 applied to
  a crash as a detector.  N1 and N3 are both W-c, deliberately:
  they are the same fault reached from the two sides of the
  equality W-c asserts, and a harness in which only one of them
  dies would mean W-c is comparing a quantity with itself.

  A FALSE POSITIVE IN THIS HARNESS'S OWN DETECTOR, on its first
  run, recorded because it is the more useful half of today's
  result.  verdict() originally matched `(W-[a-e]) FAILED`, and the
  audit's summary line read "W-a..W-e FAILED" -- so the regex
  matched "W-e FAILED" inside the summary and EVERY mutant came
  back with W-e in its killer column.  W-e detects a log-growth
  fault and is blind to all four correctness faults, so it was
  credited with four detections it did not make.  Nothing in the
  pass/fail result was wrong -- 5 of 5 were genuinely killed -- and
  the ATTRIBUTION was wrong for four of them, which is exactly the
  quantity this harness exists to report.  What gave it away was an
  arithmetic disagreement inside one row: N1 showed three W-tags
  beside UVM_ERROR=2.  Fixed in two places: the regex is anchored
  on the colon the error form always has, and the summary line no
  longer contains a W-x token.  The rule, which is new: a summary
  line must not be parseable as one of the things it summarises.

  WHAT N2 IS REALLY MEASURING, and it is a property of this
  environment rather than of the code.  N2 strips the ordinal and
  the timestamp from the witness, leaving the item text.  It is
  only caught because several cells here are closed by the SAME
  payload every time -- cp_rx_error.frame by 0xc3 and
  cp_rx_error.parity by 0x7e, three and two times respectively.
  In an environment with constrained-random payloads the item text
  would differ and N2 would SURVIVE, so W-d's strength depends on
  the stimulus it is run against.  That is recorded here rather
  than claimed away: W-d is sound (the ordinal makes duplicates
  impossible by construction) but its mutation-detection power is
  stimulus-dependent, which is the 2026-09-28 observer item in a
  new place.
