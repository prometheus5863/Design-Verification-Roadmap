=== Stage 3b: a mutant that this suite provably CANNOT catch at this depth ===
  N5 disables the STATUS read-to-clear path entirely, the defect C6
  exists to catch. It is expected to SURVIVE, and that expectation is
  asserted rather than hoped for.

  Why: after reset the three sticky error bits are 0, and the ONLY way
  to set one is for the RX engine to complete a serial frame -- a start
  bit plus eight data bits plus a stop bit at sixteen oversample ticks
  each, ~145 clocks at the fastest legal baud. bmc runs to depth 20.
  C6's antecedent is therefore never satisfiable in the bounded window,
  so C6 is VACUOUSLY TRUE here and no mutation of the clear path can
  break it. The same holds for C7.

  This is the honest form of the statement 'C6 is proved': it is proved
  only over traces the solver can reach, and it is measured here that
  those traces do not include the interesting one. uart_csr_deep_cover.sby
  is the attempt to reach it; see README.md for what that cost.
  PASS  N5 survived, as predicted -- the depth limit is real and measured
