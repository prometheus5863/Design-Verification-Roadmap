WHAT THIS MEASURES, AND THE ONE RESULT THAT WAS NOT EXPECTED
--------------------------------------------------------------

  T1 and T2 are the two directions of a stuck bit, and between them
  they exercise BOTH predictions and neither is redundant: the
  fill-phase check can only ever see tx_empty wrongly CLEAR, and the
  post-push check can only ever see it wrongly SET, because C4
  constrains burst_len to [1, 8] so the post-push occupancy is never
  zero.  Both are caught in both modes on every seed.

  T3 IS THE RESULT.  A decode asserting tx_empty one entry early is
  correct at occupancy 0 and wrong only at occupancy 1, so it needs a
  burst that pushed EXACTLY ONE byte.  Measured:

      steer=0 (pure constrained-random)  4/4 seeds detect
      steer=1 (coverage-driven steering) 2/4 seeds detect

  THE COVERAGE-DRIVEN MODE IS WORSE.  That is the opposite of what
  this script's first draft asserted -- it claimed steering reached
  burst_len = 1 'by construction' because pick_config_steered has a
  branch setting burst_len = 1.  Reading the task rather than the
  branch: burst_len is drawn randomly from [1, 8] at the TOP of
  pick_config_steered, and the coverage loops then OVERWRITE it with
  8 or 3 or 1 to chase an unhit cov_txq bin.  Two of those three
  values destroy the length-1 bursts this mutant needs, and the
  burst_len = 1 branch fires only while cov_txq[0] is unhit -- which
  it is not, since the fill phase observes an empty FIFO immediately.
  So steering can only ever REMOVE length-1 bursts here, never add
  one.

  The lesson is not about this mutant.  Coverage closure and defect
  detection are different objectives, and a generator aimed at the
  first unhit bin will spend stimulus it was otherwise spending on
  states no bin happens to name.  Occupancy 1 is such a state: cov_txq
  bins empty / partial / full, and 'partial' is hit by any burst of
  2 to 7 just as well as by a burst of 1.  THE COVERAGE MODEL CANNOT
  SEE THE DISTINCTION THE PREDICTION DEPENDS ON, so steering toward
  100 % closure is free to walk away from it.

  T4 is deliberately uninformative about the prediction.  It is
  detected in both modes, by the burst watchdog -- a data-path check
  -- because mutating the WIRE stops the engine popping the last
  byte.  Had the adjudication reported only DETECTED, this row would
  have read as a fourth success for a prediction it never tested.
